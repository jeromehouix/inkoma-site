Inkoma

Draw and animate, on iPad

Inkoma is a drawing and animation studio for iPad. The same sheet lets you draw, animate and tell stories in panels; your sheets are kept in sketchbooks.

Four modes, one sheet

Drawing

Over 190 brushes arranged by medium, each one adjustable in the brush studio. A water layer where watercolor spreads and dries on the grain of the paper. 16-bit layers with their blend modes and masks.

Every brush responds to stylus tilt, with or without pressure.

Animation

From the second frame on, the drawing comes alive: frame by frame with onion skin, along a path, as a puppet on a skeleton, or in stop motion with the iPad's camera.

Here, twelve frames at 12 fps, the two previous ones as onion skin; export to video.

Comics

Pages and panels that hold your strokes, speech balloons, sound effects, screentones; the album as a PDF. Your handwriting becomes a font, used by the balloons.

Vector

Ink that stays crisp at any zoom, shapes to combine, the pen and its handles; export to SVG and PDF.

Everything is kept in sketchbooks

Page after page, with their covers, on a shelf; a sketchbook becomes a PDF book, an animation leaves as a video.

Privacy policy

Updated: October 26, 2026

In short

Inkoma collects no personal data. The app has no account, contains no advertising, no analytics and no trackers, and connects to no server. What you draw stays on your device.

What is stored on the device

  • Your artworks: their layers, the frames of their animation, their format, their name, a thumbnail, and the photos, videos or sounds you place in them.
  • Your sketchbooks: their name, their cover, the order of their pages and what you write in them by hand.
  • Your brushes and the app's settings.
  • A technical log of artwork openings and saves, to understand a problem; it does not leave the device.

These files stay in the space reserved for the app (visible in Files). They follow the device backup (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS settings, managed by Apple. The publisher never has access to them.

Permissions

Inkoma requests a permission when a feature needs it; you can refuse it, or withdraw it at any time in Settings.

  • Camera: take photos for stop motion and draw over them.
  • Microphone: record a sound to add to an animation, such as a voice or a sound effect.
  • Photos: import the photo or video you choose, and save the images and videos you export to Photos. Inkoma reads only what you choose.
  • Files: no permission; Inkoma opens only the files you choose.

What you capture or import this way stays on the device, in the artwork where you place it; nothing is sent. Inkoma does not use your location. The tilt, orientation and pressure of the stylus, when it provides them, are used only to draw the stroke.

Sharing

An image or a video leaves the app only when you share it yourself (iOS share sheet), to the destination you choose. Inkoma sends nothing on its own.

Children

Inkoma collects no data, including children's; the app is used without an account, in class as anywhere else.

Your rights

Since no data is collected, the publisher holds nothing about you. You stay in control of your artworks: delete them from the Home screen (they spend 30 days in Recently Deleted, then are erased), or all of them by deleting the app.
